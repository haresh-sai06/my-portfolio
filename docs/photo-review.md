Status: waiting for the user's new photos and approval of previews. Existing portrait files must stay in place until approval.

1. Ask for front-facing, left and right three-quarter, profile, and upper-body photos in clear natural light. The user may provide a subset.
2. Inspect the supplied photos. Select crops that preserve the person's appearance and leave room for the portfolio text.
3. Prepare and show labeled previews for the six portrait slots and spotlight section. If generated or edited raster imagery is requested, use the ImageGen skill and tool.
4. Obtain explicit approval of the displayed replacements before installing them.
5. Save approved assets with new versioned filenames rather than overwriting cached images. Update both PortraitScroll and SpotlightReveal together, including aspect ratio and face positioning.
6. Check desktop and mobile previews, contrast, crossfades, and missing assets. Then publish the approved replacements to the existing portfolio.
